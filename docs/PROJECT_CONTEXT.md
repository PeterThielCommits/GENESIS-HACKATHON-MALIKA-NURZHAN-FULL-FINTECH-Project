TwinLedger is a proposed deterministic synthetic ledger audit benchmark. The project-supplied Master TZ v3.0.0 and execution prompt are under docs/reference; they are not organizer-issued. The checkout began at the initial README-only commit, with no prior phase context or schemas. Phase 1 and 2 PRs exist but are not merged here.
